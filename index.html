These rankings cover IPFcn competitions at Grade B (regional) or above from 2022 onward. For international competitions of the Asian Powerlifting Federation (APF) and the International Powerlifting Federation (IPF), only results from athletes representing China are included. A small number of athletes involved in anti-doping disputes are excluded.
