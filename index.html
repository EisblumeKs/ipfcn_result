国内成绩收录自 2020 年 IPFcn 成立以来 B 级（区域赛）及以上赛事的经典力量举三项和卧推举单项。国际成绩收录自 2017 年起中国代表队参加的亚洲力量举联合会（APF）和国际力量举联合会（IPF）赛事。国际赛单场成绩表涵盖各代表队，代表中国参赛的成绩计入总榜。个别涉及反兴奋剂争议的运动员未纳入统计。

Domestic results cover Classic Powerlifting and Classic Bench Press at IPFcn Grade B (regional) meets and above since IPFcn was established in 2020. International results cover Asian Powerlifting Federation (APF) and International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) meets attended by Team China from 2017 onward. International meet results include all teams. Performances for Team China count toward the overall rankings. A small number of athletes involved in anti-doping disputes are excluded.
